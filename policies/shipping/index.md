## Order Processing

Pinnacle Home Goods is operated by MTH Pinnacle LLC. Many of our products are made to order and require production time before they are shipped.

Processing and production times may vary depending on the product, destination, holidays, promotional periods, or periods of unusually high order volume.

Once an order enters production, we may not be able to modify or cancel it.

## Shipping Rates

We offer free standard shipping on orders over **$50**.

For orders that do not qualify for free shipping, available shipping rates and methods are displayed during checkout.

## Estimated Delivery

Delivery estimates depend on production time, destination, carrier, and the shipping method available for the order.

Any delivery dates or timeframes provided are estimates and are not guaranteed. Carrier delays, weather, holidays, and other circumstances outside our control may affect delivery.

## Order Tracking

When tracking information becomes available, we may send shipment details and a tracking link to the email address provided with your order.

Tracking information may take additional time to become active after a shipping label is created.

## Incorrect Shipping Information

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate shipping information during checkout.

If you discover an error in your shipping information, contact us as soon as possible at **info@mthpinnaclellc.com**. Because made-to-order products may enter production quickly, we cannot guarantee that an address or order can be changed after it has been submitted for fulfillment.

Additional shipping charges resulting from an incorrect or incomplete address may be the customer's responsibility where permitted by law.

## Lost or Delayed Shipments

If tracking indicates that your package is significantly delayed or appears to be lost, contact us at **info@mthpinnaclellc.com** with your order information.

We will review the available tracking and fulfillment information and work with our fulfillment and shipping providers as appropriate.

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, misprinted, or different from what you ordered, contact us within **30 days of delivery**.

Please include your order number, a description of the problem, and photographs of the item and packaging when applicable. Eligible issues may be resolved through a replacement or refund in accordance with our Returns & Refunds Policy.

## Contact

Questions about shipping may be sent to **info@mthpinnaclellc.com**.
