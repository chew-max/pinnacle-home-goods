## About This Policy

Pinnacle Home Goods is operated by MTH Pinnacle LLC.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process personal information when you visit our storefront, place an order, contact us, or otherwise interact with Pinnacle Home Goods.

## Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our store, we may collect information such as:

- Your name
- Email address
- Shipping and billing address
- Telephone number when provided
- Products ordered and other order details
- Transaction and payment-related information
- Shipping and tracking information
- Communications you send to customer support
- Technical information associated with your use of the storefront

We may also receive information from service providers involved in processing payments, fulfilling orders, delivering communications, hosting our services, preventing fraud, or operating the storefront.

## How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

- Process and manage orders
- Arrange production, fulfillment, and shipping
- Process payments and transaction-related activity
- Provide order confirmations, shipping notifications, and other transactional communications
- Provide customer support
- Handle replacements, refunds, disputes, or other order issues
- Maintain and improve our storefront and services
- Detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, misuse, security incidents, or other prohibited activity
- Maintain business and transaction records
- Comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations

## Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate portions of our storefront and order-processing system.

These providers may include:

- **Global Payments** for payment processing and related transaction services
- **Printify** for product production, fulfillment, and shipping
- **Resend** for transactional email delivery
- **Cloudflare** for backend infrastructure and related services
- **GitHub Pages** for storefront hosting

These providers may process personal information as necessary to perform services on our behalf or as otherwise described by their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect our legal rights, to prevent fraud or misuse, or in connection with a business transaction involving MTH Pinnacle LLC.

## Payment Information

Payments are processed through our payment service provider, Global Payments.

Payment information submitted during checkout may be transmitted to and processed by Global Payments and other parties involved in the payment transaction.

Pinnacle Home Goods receives and maintains transaction-related information necessary to process and administer orders, but payment-card processing is performed through our payment provider.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our storefront and the services supporting it may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser technologies when necessary to provide storefront functionality, maintain shopping-cart information, support checkout, protect services, or operate the website.

If analytics, advertising, or additional tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those practices.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including processing and supporting orders, maintaining business and transaction records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and satisfying legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is maintained.

## Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information handled through our storefront and backend services.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us at **info@mthpinnaclellc.com**.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests. Some information may also need to be retained when permitted or required by law, including records associated with transactions, fraud prevention, taxes, accounting, or legal obligations.

## Children's Privacy

Our storefront is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, contact us at **info@mthpinnaclellc.com**.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, business practices, providers, or legal obligations.

The current version will be made available on this page.

## Contact

Privacy-related questions or requests may be sent to **info@mthpinnaclellc.com**.
